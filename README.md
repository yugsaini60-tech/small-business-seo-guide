# Small Business SEO Guide

A practical SEO guide designed to help small businesses understand search engine optimisation, improve their website visibility, and attract relevant organic traffic through Google Search.

This guide covers essential SEO practices that small businesses can implement to build a stronger online presence and improve their chances of reaching potential customers.

## What You Will Learn

- Keyword Research
- On-Page SEO
- Technical SEO
- Local SEO
- Link Building
- Website Speed Optimisation
- Content Marketing

## SEO Checklist for Small Businesses

1. Identify relevant keywords that your potential customers search for.
2. Create useful, original, and informative website content.
3. Optimise page titles, meta descriptions, and heading structures.
4. Make your website mobile-friendly and easy to navigate.
5. Improve website loading speed and Core Web Vitals.
6. Create and optimise your Google Business Profile.
7. Build relevant backlinks through genuine industry relationships and useful resources.
8. Track website performance using Google Search Console and analytics tools.

## Why SEO Matters for Small Businesses

Search engine optimisation helps small businesses attract relevant visitors without depending entirely on paid advertising.

A well-planned SEO strategy can improve online visibility, help potential customers discover products and services, and establish long-term credibility.

For example, a local service business targeting location-specific searches can improve its visibility by publishing relevant service pages, maintaining accurate business information, and creating useful content around customer queries.

SEO requires consistency, useful content, technical improvements, and regular performance monitoring.
